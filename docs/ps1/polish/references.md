# White City and Jaffa Port study

The owner requested more varied White City buildings and a Jaffa Port influence on
Sea Walk. This extends ART-09's animation/daylight/entrance/context polish. Photographs
were inspected in the browser; no photograph or AI image is included in the game assets.

## Sources inspected

- [UNESCO — White City of Tel-Aviv](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1096/):
  modernist variety and adaptation to the coastal climate, including entrance and window details.
- [Germany's international portal — preserving the White City](https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/culture/100-years-of-the-bauhaus-preserving-the-white-city-in-tel-aviv):
  the facade photograph shows rounded balcony corners, continuous pale parapets and deep
  horizontal shade. These informed the rounded balcony mesh and recessed upper walls.
- [Tel Aviv's visitor site — Jaffa Port](https://www.visit-tel-aviv.com/en/jaffa-port/):
  context for a working harbor rather than a generic beach promenade.
- [Jaffa waterfront photograph in Plum Guide's Tel Aviv guide](https://www.plumguide.com/journal/tel-aviv-travel-guide):
  the Getty photograph shows warm stone buildings stepping up behind the quay, arches,
  blue/green fishing hulls, dark fenders, mooring ropes and compact wheelhouses.
- [Michael Jacobson's photographic walk through Jaffa Port](https://michaelarch.wordpress.com/2012/11/28/סיבוב-בנמל-יפו-המתחדש-וביפו-העתיקה/):
  the waterfront warehouse and fishing-boat photograph informed a low corrugated shed,
  faded painted bands and working decks. Retain the industrial side of the port.

## Translation into original Sela assets

Five facade types share a restrained plaster palette but differ in silhouette: curved
balconies, recessed loggia, stairwell glazing, roof terrace, and a taller curved variant.
The home, Kettle and laundrette each use a different type. Background blocks vary in
height, width and setback. Side windows matter because the camera is diagonal.

Sea Walk uses coursed warm stone, true voussoir arches, shuttered storehouses and stepped
roof rooms behind the existing stair exit. Five small fishing boats, a low shed, rope
coils and a distant rock breakwater establish a working harbor. These are compressed
original compositions, not replicas of specific landmarks.

Existing map data, collision, doors, review-board access and save coordinates are unchanged.
New context lives beyond the playable footprint; its presence does not imply new routes.
