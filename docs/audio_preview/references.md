# Revision 02 reference notes

The owner specified Hikaru no Go OST 1 track 07 **Majiwaru Michi**, OST 1 track 23 **Shukuteki**, and OST 2 track 26 **Mezame**, alongside a sharp **THWACK** for stone contact.

Sources checked:

- [TV Tokyo’s original series music catalog](https://www.tv-tokyo.co.jp/anime/hikaru2001/goods/main.html) distinguishes the original score albums from the opening/ending song releases.
- [OST 1 track listing](https://colettecdconnection.blogspot.com/2012/11/hikaru-no-go-original-soundtrack.html) identifies tracks 07 and 23, including the longer first cue and shorter rivalry cue.
- [OST 2 catalog](https://www.cdjapan.co.jp/product/AVCA-14576) includes track 26, 目覚め (Mezame).
- [Shukuteki drum arrangement](https://www.songsterr.com/a/wsa/hikaru-no-go-hikaru-no-go-shukuteki-ost-23-drum-tab-s2503601) is a fan transcription, not an official orchestration.

These sources establish the reference set; they do not constitute an aural comparison of the recordings. No claim is made to have listened to or exactly reconstructed these tracks. The revision interprets the owner’s requested dramatic quality through a strong recurring melody, lyrical piano/woodwind/string writing in the room, a repeating bass figure and active strings during competition, and a brighter later statement. The composition and note sequences are original.

No anime audio, melody transcription or downloaded soundtrack file enters the build. The sound sources remain the pinned Kenney contact recordings and GeneralUser instruments. Human listening should establish whether this interpretation is actually close to the intended feeling; the prior pass remains available for comparison.
