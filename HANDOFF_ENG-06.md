## The problem, as the owner experiences it

The owner, a beginner, played the shipped game after M40 and was crushed in every game,
including against Abel (labelled 21 kyu, the weakest character) and Wren (20 kyu). Rule 3
in `CLAUDE.md` says every opponent's label is a real rank, and the whole first act is built

Independent evidence from the M40 session: the autopilot's player brain — the shipped
heuristic (`src/go_ai/heuristic_opponent.gd`) at `mistake_rate` 0, `reading_depth` 2,
which the game labels 1 dan — lost to Abel's profile by 43 points and, at its 12 kyu
setting, to Wren's by 86. So either the KataGo profiles play well above their labels, or
the heuristic played well below its own. The owner's games say it is the former, at least
in part.

## Where the strength comes from

Every character profile (`data/opponents/*.tres`, generated by `tools/gen_content.py`)
runs KataGo with the Human-SL model and a generated config
`packaging/katago/config/human_<rank>_<style>.cfg` (steady / balanced / fighting). The
relevant lines of `human_20k_steady.cfg`:

```
maxVisits = 8
humanSLProfile = preaz_20k
humanSLChosenMoveProp = 1.0
humanSLChosenMovePiklLambda = 100000000
chosenMoveTemperatureEarly = 0.65
chosenMoveTemperature = 0.45
chosenMoveTemperatureHalflife = 80
```

KataGo's own notes on these knobs (`gtp_human5k_example.cfg` from the v1.15 release; a
copy is at commit `ccaea8c` on `feat/core-route-facelift`, or in the KataGo release zip):

- With `humanSLChosenMoveProp = 1.0` and a huge `PiklLambda`, the search is ignored for
  move choice; visits only matter for pass/resign judgement. So `maxVisits = 8` is not
  the strength knob.
- **Temperature is.** KataGo's comment: a low `chosenMoveTemperature` makes the engine play
  the *majority vote* of players at that rank, which "would avoid a lot of blunders that
  players of that level would make". The example config uses 0.85 early / 0.70; ours uses
  0.65 / 0.45, and the "steady" temperament lowers it further. A "20 kyu" that never makes
  a 20 kyu's blunders is not a 20 kyu. This is the prime suspect.
- The `preaz_<rank>` profiles were trained on online games; KataGo warns the model is out
  of distribution for large rank gaps and mismatched handicaps.

The temperaments live in `tools/gen_content.py` (`style_overrides`, around line 295) and
`tools/katago_style_probe.gd`; three UI fixtures `katago_style_*.json` exist.

## What to do

1. **Measure before touching anything.** Build a headless strength probe (start from
   `tools/katago_calibrate.gd` and `tools/katago_review_test.gd`, which already play whole
   games headlessly and talk to the engine): play N full 9×9 games per beginner profile
   (Abel 21k, Wren 20k, Dov 19k, Pip 18k, Moss 16k, Kesh 12k) against a fixed reference
   and record the score margins. Candidate references, cheapest first: the same Human-SL
   model at temperature 1.0 as a "realistic 20 kyu"; the heuristic at several settings;
   GNU Go level 0 (about 8 kyu) if it can be installed under `steam-run`. Also record
   `chosenMoveTemperature` sensitivity: the same profile at 0.45, 0.70, 1.0.
2. **Decide the tolerance with the owner** (the ticket is `NEEDS DECISION`): how far a
   profile may sit from its label. Propose one; do not choose silently.
3. **Retune.** Most likely: raise the temperatures towards KataGo's defaults (1.0 is the
   "realistic individual" setting; 0.70/0.85 is the example), regenerate the configs with
   `python3 tools/gen_content.py`, re-measure, and re-run `tools/katago_calibrate.gd` so
   latency and legality still hold (the M39 gate: under two seconds a reply, no fallbacks).
4. **Prove it at the keyboard** (`CLAUDE.md` rule 9). The autopilot cannot play like a
   beginner, so the evidence is the owner playing Abel and Wren and winning some. The
   fixtures `review_world_wren_loss` / `review_world_wren` (`tools/autopilot/`) play whole
   games through the town and show the result card; use them for regressions, not for
   strength.
5. **Sweep the documents** as `CLAUDE.md` demands: `WORKBOARD.md` (ENG-06 evidence and
   status), `ROADMAP.md` §1, `MILESTONES.md` (a new entry with the measured margins before
   and after, and a correction under M39 saying what its calibration did and did not
   measure), `CLAUDE.md` if the temperament or rank story changes.

## Things that will bite

- `run_game.sh` takes an exclusive lock and every fixture overwrites save slot 1.
- Long fixture batches launched with a background tool were killed when the session's
  turn ended; launch them with `setsid nohup … &` and poll in the foreground.
- `pkill -f <pattern>` matches your own shell's command line. Kill by pid.
- `godot --headless … --script` runs have no autoloads; anything a test needs must live on
  a pure class (`src/go/`, `src/go_ai/`). `KataGoAnalysis`, `MatchAnalysis` and
  `GtpOpponent` are usable there; `MatchReviewService` is not.
- The KataGo binary and models are not in git: `tools/setup_katago.sh --verify`.
- One KataGo evaluation costs about a core-second on this CPU build; a 9×9 game is
  60–80 positions. Budget probe runs accordingly and print timings.

## Do not

- Do not change rank labels to match the engine. Rule 3 says the labels are real ranks;
  the engine must be made to play them.
- Do not fix it by lowering `maxVisits`; per KataGo's notes it is not the strength knob
  here, and visits are what keep pass/resign judgement sane.
- Do not touch the review (M40) or the heuristic's labels as part of this ticket unless
  the measurement says the heuristic is the mislabelled one; if so, file it separately.
